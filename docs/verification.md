# Current verification

Use the commands in the current game/tool guides and required CI. A changed
toolchain requires new executions; past measurements do not become current by
editing their version numbers. Source availability, a successful build, a finite
test, graphical startup and human play acceptance are different evidence.

The current maintenance run is recorded in `CURRENT-VERIFICATION.md` after its
consumer checks. Linux/macOS CI, Wasm builds, real editors and long campaigns
remain unqualified unless their own result is recorded explicitly.
